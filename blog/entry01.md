The topic I decided on for this project is arts because I have always been passionate about this subject. I like this subject because its a form of expression and character and there are many ways to express yourself, and the arts just so happen to be one of them. Another website I use for inspiration/information on the arts and more about it is [creating creativity](https://medium.com/@CellestialStudios/creating-creativity-4c6579bec775) which provides information and guiding steps to the creation or beginning of arts.
