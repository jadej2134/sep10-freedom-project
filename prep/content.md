# Below are some apps that help actors rehearse,etc.

## ColdRead
* [coldRead](https://coldreadapp.com/#c_app_howitworks)
* This app helps learning or upcoming actors rehearse their lines or practice with a bot.
* This app also helps people rehearse their lines or fix the lines they may be stuck on.
* You can also adjust the settings on the app to your level of comfort or in a pace you can work with.

## WeAudition
* [WeAudition](https://www.weaudition.com/)
  * This app helps actors rehearse for audtions.
  * Most reviews state how they made money off being a reader for others on the app.
  * Real-Time hires for running lines with others.

## Final Draft
* [FinalDraft](https://www.finaldraft.com/?__hstc=43231290.dcd3a3ea70fdfdab539a91aeb76f564b.1760704852565.1760997173298.1761568202614.3&__hssc=43231290.1.1761568202614&__hsfp=288830304)  
  * This app helps writers create plots for ideas they have, they can also collaborate with other writers.
  * The app also contains files where you can store your work and write down your ideas.
  * The app also contains built-in commenting that can give the writers feedback on what they could improvise.

## Table Read
* [TableRead](https://www.tablereadpro.com/)
  * This app allows actors to download /lisetn to plays by turning a script into a PDF form.
  * This app allows the actors to add any effects(stage directions) to the script or audio to get through the script easier or for better directions if they're stuck.
  * Good for rehearsal and customs
